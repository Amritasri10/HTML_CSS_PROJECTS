about us

explore the difference

Backroads was built by travellers, for travellers. We specialise in small-group tours that take you deep into local culture — staying in family-run guesthouses, eating at street markets, and hiking trails that most tour buses never find.

Every itinerary is designed by our team of seasoned explorers who have lived in and travelled through each destination. We keep group sizes under 12 so every experience stays personal, flexible, and genuinely memorable.
